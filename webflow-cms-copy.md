## 6 FAQs

### FAQ 1
**Title:** Is this TikTok caption generator free?
**Body:** Yes. The TikTok caption generator is completely free and needs no signup or account. Describe your video, pick a tone, and generate as many times as you like.

### FAQ 2
**Title:** How many captions do I get?
**Body:** Every run gives you five distinct captions written in the tone you choose, plus a separate hashtag set. You can regenerate for a fresh batch anytime.

### FAQ 3
**Title:** Does it include hashtags?
**Body:** Yes. Alongside the five captions you get a matching set of eight to twelve hashtags that mix broad and specific tags for your topic, with its own copy button.

### FAQ 4
**Title:** Can I use these captions for Reels or Shorts too?
**Body:** You can. The captions are written to be short and punchy, which works well for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. You may want to adjust the hashtags to fit each platform.

### FAQ 5
**Title:** Will the captions sound generic?
**Body:** The generator writes to the specific video description and tone you give it, and it varies the angle across the five options. The more detail you add about your video, the more tailored the captions will be.

### FAQ 6
**Title:** How should I choose the right tone?
**Body:** Match the tone to the feeling of your video. A skincare routine might suit aesthetic or soft, a hot take suits bold, and a behind the scenes clip suits relatable or storytime. Try a couple of tones and compare the results.
